# CivicPulse: User manual

Screenshots of every page are in [screenshots/](screenshots/).

## Getting started
1. Open the site and choose **Create account**. Public sign-up always creates a citizen account.
2. Sign in with your email and password. You land on your home page: **Dashboard** (citizen), **My tasks** (staff) or **Overview** (admin).
3. On a phone, the menu is behind the menu button at the top left.

## Citizens
- **Report an issue:** choose **Report issue**. Fill in the title, description, category and a landmark. Click the map (or drag the pin, or press **Use my location**) to set the exact spot. Add a photo if you have one (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB).
  - While you type, a **Recommendation** box may suggest a category and show similar open reports nearby. If one of those is your issue, support it from the community list instead of reporting it again.
- **Track:** **My reports** lists your reports with their tracking code (for example `CP-2611-0001`). Open one to see its timeline, photos and comments.
- **Support:** **Community** lists open reports from others. Press the support button once to show the problem affects you too.
- **After it's fixed:** when a report is resolved, choose **Yes, it is fixed**, or **Reopen report** with a note if it isn't.
- **Notifications:** the bell shows how many updates you haven't read.

## Staff
- **My tasks** shows how many tasks are to start, in progress and awaiting verification, plus the task list.
- Open a task and press **Start work**. Add **progress notes** as you go.
- Upload at least one **completion evidence** photo, then press **Submit for verification**.
- If the admin sends it back, you'll get a notification with the reason. Continue the work and submit again.

## Admins
- **Overview:** live figures, charts, overdue reports, hotspots and staff workload. **Export all reports (CSV)** downloads a spreadsheet.
- **All issues:** search, filter, sort and export the current results.
- **Issue page actions:**
  - **Start review**
  - **Assign** or **Reassign** to a staff member (open task counts are shown)
  - Set the **priority**
  - **Request more information** from the reporter
  - **Reject** with a reason
  - **Merge** a duplicate into another tracking code
  - **Verify and resolve**, or **Send back** to staff
- **Recommendations panel:** a suggested category and priority (with the reasons) and possible duplicates. These are rule-based suggestions: check them, then use **Apply** or **Merge into it** if you agree.
- **Staff:** create staff accounts (a one-time password is shown once; share it securely), change roles, and deactivate accounts. Staff with open tasks must have them reassigned first.

## Troubleshooting
| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "Map tiles could not be loaded" | No internet for map images. Pins and coordinates still work. |
| "Too many attempts" (429) | Rate limit. Wait a minute and try again. |
| "Page not found" for an issue | It doesn't exist, or your account isn't allowed to see it. |
